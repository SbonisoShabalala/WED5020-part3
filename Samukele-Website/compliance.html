SAFETY FIRST

Preparation is part of protection.

Abnormal load movements require case-specific checks. The client and operators should confirm permits, route permissions, vehicle suitability and escort requirements before travel.

01

Before the move

Review dimensions, mass, route restrictions, planned timing and the responsible authorities.

02

On the road

Coordinate communication, vehicle visibility and the approved movement plan.

03

Documentation

Keep applicable permits and supporting documents available for the operation.
