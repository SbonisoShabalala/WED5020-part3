
PROPOSAL 1: Samukele Trading and Projects (Pty) Ltd 

Category: Small Business - Transport & Logistics 

1. Project Overview: Samukele Trading and Projects currently has no website. The proposed website will create a professional, trustworthy online presence to showcase their abnormal load escort services, safety compliance, and allow clients to request quotes online. 

2. Goals and Objectives 

Build credibility and trust with corporate clients 

Showcase services: route planning, traffic management, escort vehicles 

Make it easy for clients to request a quote and contact the team 

Display certifications, safety compliance and service areas 

Improve visibility on Google for "abnormal load escort SA" 

3. Target Audience Transport companies, construction firms, mining companies, and logistics managers searching for accredited escort services in South Africa. 

4. Proposed Sitemap / Pages 

Home - Hero banner, who we are, why choose us, call-to-action "Request a Quote" 

About Us - Company story, mission, safety focus 

Services - Abnormal load escort, Route planning, Traffic management 

Compliance & Safety - Certifications, regulations, safety procedures 

Service Areas - Map of SA coverage 

Gallery - Fleet and escort operations 

Request a Quote - Form with load details, date, route 

Contact Us - Form, phone, WhatsApp, location 

5. Features and Functionality 

Responsive design (mobile, tablet, desktop) 

Quote request form with email integration 

WhatsApp chat button for instant inquiries 

SEO optimized pages 

Google Maps integration 

Testimonials section 

Downloadable company profile PDF 

6. Design Concept  

Professional, bold and safety focused. Colors: Yellow (safety), Black, Grey and White. Strong fonts, clear icons for services, images of trucks, and escort vehicles. Layout will be clean and corporate. 

7. Technology Stack 

Front-end: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript 

Framework: WordPress OR custom HTML/CSS/JS (depending on client) 

Forms: PHP / Form handling 

Hosting: Affordable hosting with SSL certificate 

8. Benefits to the Organisation The website will help Samukele attract more corporate clients, look more professional than competitors without websites, and convert online searches into quote requests. 

PROPOSAL 2: Men Mental Health SA 

Category: Non-Profit Organisation 

1. Project Overview Men Mental Health SA has no website. The proposed website will be a safe, supportive, and accessible online platform where men can find information, get help anonymously, and join support programmes. 

2. Goals and Objectives 

Break the stigma around men seeking help 

Provide easy access to mental health resources 

Allow users to book counseling, join support groups, and register for workshops 

Create a safe community through forums and stories 

Enable donations and volunteer sign-ups 

3. Target Audience Men of all ages (especially 18-40), students, working professionals, plus families, schools, and workplaces that support men's mental health. 

4. Proposed Sitemap / Pages 

Home - Calming hero message "It's Okay To Talk", helpline number, quick help buttons 

About Us - Mission, vision, team 

Understanding Mental Health - Articles on stress, depression, anxiety 

Get Help - Counseling referrals, emergency contacts, self-assessment quiz 

Support Groups & Events - Calendar, registration forms 

Resources - Videos, podcasts, downloadable guides 

Stories - Anonymous testimonials from men 

Donate / Volunteer - Payment gateway, volunteer form 

Contact Us - Confidential contact form, 24/7 helpline 

5. Features and Functionality 

Calm, private and user-friendly design 

Anonymous "Ask for Help" form 

Event booking system 

Resource library (filter by topic) 

Blog for awareness articles 

Donation integration (Paystack / PayPal) 

Emergency pop-up with helpline numbers (SADAG) 

High privacy and data protection 

6. Design Concept Empathetic, calm and modern. Colors: Deep Blue (trust), Teal, White, and soft Grey. Lots of white space, friendly imagery of diverse South African men, easy-to-read fonts. Tone: supportive, non-judgmental. 

7. Technology Stack 

Front-end: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript 

CMS: WordPress (for easy content updates by NPO staff) 

Plugins: Event calendar, donation plugin, secure forms 

Hosting: Secure hosting with SSL and daily backups 

8. Benefits to the Organisation The website will give Men Mental Health SA a central hub to reach men nationally, make help-seeking less intimidating, increase workshop attendance, and attract donors and partners. 

 

Conclusion Both organisations will greatly benefit from a professional website. For Samukele Trading, the focus is on trust and lead generation. For Men Mental Health SA, the focus is on accessibility, support and community.
